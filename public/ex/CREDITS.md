# Exercise illustrations

The drawings in this folder come from the **Everkinetic** open exercise data set,
created by Greg Priday (everkinetic.com) and maintained at
https://github.com/everkinetic/data.

License: **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes made for Liftlog: cropped to the figure, resized to 560 px, and recoloured
from black-on-white to white line art on a transparent background (WebP). These
adapted images are shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.

Each exercise has two files: `<id>-a.webp` (stretched position, Everkinetic's
"relaxation" frame) and `<id>-b.webp` (contracted position, the "tension" frame).

The exercise guides (steps and tips) in `lib/exercises.ts` are written for Liftlog
and are not part of the Everkinetic data.
